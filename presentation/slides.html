The problem

Superfluid announces nothing
while the money drains.

A treasury pays salaries as streams. Money leaves the account every second — and there is no event, no log to index, no threshold crossing to watch.

  • When the available balance reaches zero, anyone may liquidate the sender and take the locked buffer.
  • Every stream from that treasury collapses in the same block. Not the smallest first, not the least important — all of them, at once.
  • Payroll stops for everyone, with no warning, and the first person to notice is the one who was not paid.

2

The design decision everything rests on

The agent holds a mandate,
not the keys.

Runway never holds the treasury's private key and cannot be given it. It operates through Superfluid's own access-control list — granted in one transaction, revoked in one transaction, enforced by the CFA contract rather than by Runway's code.

permissions = 6

Update and delete only — deliberately not 7. The contract reverts a createFlow from this operator. It can throttle or close a stream; it can never open one to an address of its own choosing.

A flow-rate allowance

Set to the sum of the committed rates. It can restore what was agreed and cannot exceed it.

Revocable without us

The treasury calls the same function with permissions = 0. Runway's cooperation is not required, and that is the point.

What one tick does

Read

Balance, deposit, and every stream's live rate, off the chain. If any read fails, the tick fails — it never decides on partial facts.

Decide

A pure function of those facts and a policy the treasury owns. Shed discretionary first, then standard, then critical — each stopping at its floor.

Execute

One superfluid/update-flow per adjusted stream through KeeperHub, every parameter literal, each carrying an idempotency key derived from the work rather than from the attempt.

Verify

Read every adjusted stream back. A mined receipt proves a transaction landed; only a read proves the stream changed rate.

Escalate

On an incomplete read, a rejected mandate, an unknown write outcome, or a budget the floors cannot meet. Silence is never the answer to a case the policy does not cover.

Why you can believe those numbers

The evidence document is parsed,
not written.

pnpm test:evidence reads every hash, link, block number and rate back out of the captured run record, and fails if the document disagrees.

  • The gate shipped red. The document carried a sentinel no real value could equal, so no number could be written into it before a real run produced one.
  • That is the difference between a figure in a README and a figure someone can check — and it is the only reason a reader should trust the slide before this one.

What it refuses to do

Guess, choose for you, or go quiet.

  • Reads fail closed. A partial read raises an error listing every failure. A treasury keeper that guesses when the chain is unreachable is worse than one that stops.
  • It never cuts below a floor. When the floors together still exceed the budget, it does not decide who matters least — it stops and hands the remainder to a human.
  • An escalation that was not delivered is recorded as not delivered. Posted to the policy's webhook, retried with a widening delay under a hard timeout, and the delivery outcome written down either way.
  • It cannot pay anyone new. Not as a policy — as a contract permission it was never granted.
